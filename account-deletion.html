Delete your Gradiometer account

This page permanently deletes your Gradiometer Firebase account, profile, community posts and uploaded circuit images.

Direct web deletion is available for accounts that have an email/password sign-in linked to them. If you use an anonymous account, open Gradiometer and use Settings → Delete my account, or contact support below so we can verify your request.

Need help?

The deletion request is processed after account ownership is verified. Some records may be retained where legally required.
